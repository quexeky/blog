Alright so I'm starting Uni soon - as of right now, [131h 20m 50s](https://datayze.com/time-until) until my first class. And I'm not really sure what I think just yet. Of course, I've been to O-Week a little bit already - seen a few of the clubs / societies, debated which projects I want to join (Out of BlueSat, Rocketry, and Redback Racing, I think I'm going to go for Redback if I get an offer). The interesting bit though is just how underwhelmed I think I have been. I keep asking people for more things about everything, and most of the things that people tell me, I already know. I guess at this point I'm more going to O-Week for networking than anything else. I've already met a few people though which was nice.

Another thing that I hadn't realised was just how much pre-uni work there was. To be fair I was expecting maybe 20 minutes or something, but instead I've already gotten a bunch of Week 1 quizzes and such from Physics the Uni itself. But oh well - at least I'll be a little ahead.

Back to O-Week though, I still haven't found anyone else studying EE just yet who wasn't at a society stand. Where on earth do y'all hang out? Or are EE students like faeries (faries?) that appear when it's least convenient. Damn do I really want to find a good group for my DESN1000 course.

Another thing that I've been doing in preparation for Uni is actually setting up [Paperless-NGX](https://docs.paperless-ngx.com/), along with [Paperless-GPT](https://github.com/icereed/paperless-gpt) and a few other bits and bobs. My ideal scenario with this (which I still have yet to get working because damn you Epson) is that I can simply come home from Uni, take all of my used pages out of my binder, put them into my scanner, and then have them automatically uploaded to the aforementioned Paperless-NGX instance (hosted on Oracle's Always Free tier ofc) where they have Google DocAI attempt to annotate my handwriting, then be automatically tagged, labelled, etc, and eventually put into my searchable database. It's amazing (theoretically). But for some reason it would seem that Epson absolutely insists on being incompetent, and while my printer is capable of creating the scanned file, it never actually uploads any data. I am stuck with a big fat zero in place of the number of bytes which are meant to be uploaded. I might make a post about it once I've figured it all out.
